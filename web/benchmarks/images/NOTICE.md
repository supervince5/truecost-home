This hackathon evaluation is educational. These photographs are **not** covered by the repository's MIT license. Obtain appropriate rights before reuse beyond those terms. They are not model-training data.

Photographer attribution is also visible in the [official January 2021 EPA archive](https://19january2021snapshot.epa.gov/mold/mold-image-library_.html). The original four were retrieved October 24, 2026. The additional six were retrieved October 25, 2026; their archive URLs and checksums are in [vision-cases.json](../vision-cases.json). Model inputs contain only image bytes and application-assigned source IDs, never these captions or expected answers.

The images represent educational examples, not independently inspected homes or Redfin listings. A photograph's caption may describe facts that are not visible; only visible observations are expected from the image-only run. Inspection context, exact cause, current moisture, and concealed damage are intentionally withheld.
