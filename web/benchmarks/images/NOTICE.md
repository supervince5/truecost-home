# Educational image attribution

These four unmodified source photographs come from the [EPA Mold Image Library](https://www.epa.gov/mold/mold-image-library). EPA permits use in presentations and for educational purposes. This hackathon evaluation is educational. These photographs are **not** covered by the repository's MIT license. Obtain appropriate rights before reuse beyond those terms. They are not model-training data.

Photographer attribution is also visible in the [official January 2021 EPA archive](https://19january2021snapshot.epa.gov/mold/mold-image-library_.html). Retrieved October 24, 2026. Model inputs contain only image bytes and application-assigned source IDs, never these captions or expected answers.

The images represent educational examples, not four independently inspected homes or Redfin listings. A photograph's caption may describe facts that are not visible; only visible observations are expected from the image-only run. Inspection context, exact cause, current moisture, and concealed damage are intentionally withheld.
